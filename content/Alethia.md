Dubbed "The Bard of Konoren," Alethia grew up as an orphan on the streets of [[Konoren City]], often stealing to make it by. Eventually, after being arrested, the Konoren Government would offer her a chance at freedom, for both herself, and the poor of Konoren. So she was sent forth on a faux humanitarian mission meant truly to begin a revolution in [[Brudia City]]. Accompanied by [[Edward Fulton]] and [[Lucy]], this mission would eventually be abandoned, and the three would go forth to stop [[The Final Judgement]].

After her adventures in Brudia, Alethia would return to Konoren and become an influential member of the Konoren Thieves Guild, making herself the most wanted criminal in Konoren City, but a legend to those living in the very streets she used to barely get by in.

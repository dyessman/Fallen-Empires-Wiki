Not to be confused with their [[Panazian Warforged|Panazian counterparts]], the Warforged of Casmia are the creations of [[Syllen Brudia]]. Most were mindless drones meant only to serve [[The Final Judgement]], however one notable exception was [[Cutlass]], the head of the [[Union of Brudia Overview|Brudia]] Military and former associate of [[The Brudia Family]].

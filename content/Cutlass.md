Head of the [[Union of Brudia Overview|Brudia]] Military, and former personal friend of the [[The Brudia Family]], Cutlass is a influential figure in the political landscape of Brudia. The Brudia Senate often asks him for input on current issues and Senator Denlorn is often seen close by his side. 

After [[The Brudia Crisis]], he was said to go into an existential crisis of sorts after seeing dark corruptions of himself burning the city he helped build. His public image remained unscarred and he was still often seen with the Senate, however he was notably less outspoken in public environments.

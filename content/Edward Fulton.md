Formerly a Knight of [[Fulchrius]], Edward Fulton would find himself on a humanitarian mission to [[Brudia City]] on behalf of [[Konoren City]] alongside [[Lucy]] and [[Alethia]]. Though it would be revealed a hoax, Edward would go forth to accomplish his goals regardless as him and his companions would prevent [[The Final Judgement]]. This journey would lead him to disavowing his place as a Fulchrian Knight, and eventually would become a Paladin of [[Cas]]. 

After his journey's in Brudia, Edward would take [[Kai Brudia]] under his wing to teach him to be a Paladin like himself. The two would bond easily as they both sought to reclaim their family's legacies for the better, and move beyond their dark pasts.

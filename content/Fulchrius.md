Fulchrius was the Casmian God of Justice, before sometime in the 2nd Era when he was cast out from the Casmian Pantheon for inciting a revolt against [[Cas]]. During the 4th Era his followers, particularly [[Syllen Brudia]], began [[The Final Judgement]], a plan to return Fulchrius to power and allow him to "enact justice" on [[Casmia]]. His ultimate fate after the failure of his return is unknown.

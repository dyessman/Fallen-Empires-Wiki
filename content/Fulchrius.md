Fulchrius was the Casmian God of Justice, before sometime in the 2nd Era when he was cast out from the Casmian Pantheon for inciting a revolt against [[Cas]]. Though his followers still retain divine powers from his domain, he has been notably less direct, taking on the ways of Panazian gods by communicating to his followers with visions and symbols.

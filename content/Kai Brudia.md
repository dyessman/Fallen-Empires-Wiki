Son of [[Syllen Brudia]] and [[Naomi Brudia]], Kai would find himself as a frog nicknamed "Annoying" which accompanied [[The Chosen of Cas]] for much of their time in [[Brudia City]]. Once The Chosen began to uncover [[The Final Judgement]], he turned back into a half-elven child, and helped The Chosen stop his father. 

After his father was thwarted, Kai, the last known member of [[The Brudia Family]], was taken in by [[Edward Fulton]], and trained to be a Paladin of [[Cas]].

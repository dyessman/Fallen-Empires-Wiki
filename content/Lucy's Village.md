Home of [[Lucy]], this village would come to be the epicenter of a conflict between [[Edward Fulton]]'s father and the Parents of [[Alethia]], who were attempting to keep her away from the Knight's of [[Fulchrius]]. This conflict would lead to the village being burned. It would eventually begin to rebuild once Lucy returned and started her Library near the village's remains.

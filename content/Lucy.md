Lucy is a [[Kobold]] from a [[Lucy's Village|small village]] in [[League of Free-Cities Overview|The League of Free Cities]]. A cleric of [[Mephina]] and a scholar, Lucy would set forth on a humanitarian mission to [[Brudia City]] on behalf of [[Konoren City]] alongside [[Edward Fulton]] and [[Alethia]]. Though it would be revealed a hoax, Lucy would go forth to accomplish her goals regardless as her and her companions would prevent [[The Final Judgement]].

After her adventures in Brudia, Lucy would return home to start a library renowned throughout [[Panazia]], and become a lead researcher on the mysterious origins of [[Panazian Warforged]], her curiosity caused by her encounters with [[Casmian Warforged]].

Merog the Harpy is the [[Orcs|Orcish]] author of The [[Song of Poltroons]]. Though their name is the one attached to the songs creation, little is known about them. According to the people of the village of [[Snowhaven]], Merog was a native to the village who became one of it's three saviors after going forth on the adventure which inspired the song.

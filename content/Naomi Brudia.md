Naomi Brudia was the Matriarch of [[The Brudia Family]] and a [[The Formation of the Union of Brudia|critical founder of The Union of Brudia]]. An Elven war vet of [[The Second Colonial War]], she married [[Syllen Brudia]] and had her two children, her daughter [[Valna Brudia]] and son [[Kai Brudia]]. When Syllen began his experiments with necromancy in attempt to save the dying Valna, she disappeared, leaving her family behind completely. To this day, her fate is a mystery.

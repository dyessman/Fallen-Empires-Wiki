 The Warforged of Panazia, not to be confused with their [[Casmian Warforged|Casmian counterparts]], are a rare mechanical wonder that typically takes a humanoid shape. How they came about is unclear, and there is great mystery behind their origin. [[Coil]] is a notable Panazian Warforged as they were the first to have interacted with humanity. Over time more have come to join society but they remain a rarity in [[Panazia]].

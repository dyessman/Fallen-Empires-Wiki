A village hidden away in the [[Panazia]] Mountains, it is home to a united [[Kobold]] and [[Orcs|Orcish]] population, an increasingly common union in the lands of Panazia. It is also a recent destination of many historians and bards seeking the truth behind The [[Song of Poltroons]].

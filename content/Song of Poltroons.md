The Song of Poltroons is a Panazian folk song created within the 4th Era. Rumored to originate from an [[Merog the Harpy|Orc Bard]] from the mountains, many claim it is in fact a true recounting of the bard's encounter with a cult. Though whether the bard actually exists is a source of question. 
The song is often sung as an opening act, as it's gimmick revolves around the participation of the audience potentially saving the "heroes." Some claim there are lost alternative lyrics which actually lead to the heroes being successful, though none are written in any bardic library.

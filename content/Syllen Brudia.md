Syllen Brudia, patriarch of [[The Brudia Family]], and critical founder of [[Union of Brudia Overview|The Union of Brudia]], was a Human man and veteran of [[The Second Colonial War]]. After meeting his wife, an Elven woman named [[Naomi Brudia]], and fellow war vet, he began a pilgrimage to the western lands of [[Casmia]]. He would go on to [[The Formation of the Union of Brudia|found the Union of Brudia]] and [[Brudia City]], as well as have two children with Naomi. Shortly after the Union rose to prominence, his daughter, [[Valna Brudia]], would fall deathly ill. In response he would begin turning himself into an automaton lich, magically suspend Valna to prevent her death, and turn his son, [[Kai Brudia]], into a frog to save him from the trauma of his family separating. This would drive Naomi away and lead him to beginning [[The Final Judgement]], as [[Fulchrius]] promised in return he would help save Valna. Ultimately this would prove to be his undoing, as he would be brought to an end by [[The Chosen of Cas]].

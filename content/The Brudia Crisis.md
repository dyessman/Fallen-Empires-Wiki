A result of [[The Final Judgement]], The Brudia Crisis occurred when an army of [[Casmian Warforged|constructs]] attacked [[Brudia City]] in mass. Though eventually thwarted by [[The Chosen of Cas]], the crisis did considerable damage, especially in the poorest of districts.

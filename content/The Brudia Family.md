The founding family of [[Union of Brudia Overview|The Union of Brudia]], the Brudia Family was instrumental in the foundation of both [[Brudia City]] and the nation of Brudia. The family was notably a marriage between a Human male, [[Syllen Brudia]], and a Elven woman, [[Naomi Brudia]], symbolically showing a culture willing to move past [[The First Colonial War]] and [[The Second Colonial War]]. They would go on to have two Half-Elven children, being their daughter, [[Valna Brudia]] and their son, [[Kai Brudia]]. Some time after the nation rose to prominence, the family disappeared. It would later be revealed that Syllen had driven his wife away after experimenting on necromancy and turning himself into a lich, in a misguided attempt to save Valna from a life threatening illness.

The name given to the three heroes who stopped [[The Final Judgement]], these three are said to have been chosen by the gods themselves to save [[Casmia]] as a whole from [[Fulchrius]].

The heroes consist of:

[[Lucy]] The Kobold Scholar

[[Alethia]] The Bard of Konoren

[[Edward Fulton]] The Knight of Justice

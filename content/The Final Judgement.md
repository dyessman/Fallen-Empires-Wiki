Headed by a corrupted [[Syllen Brudia]], The Final Judgement was meant to be [[Fulchrius]]'s return to power as the God of Justice, however it was thwarted by [[The Chosen of Cas]]. It involved necromantic magics and an army of [[Casmian Warforged|constructs built by Syllen]]. It was chased by Syllen in attempts to save [[Valna Brudia|his ill daughter]].

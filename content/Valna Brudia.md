Daughter of [[Naomi Brudia]] and [[Syllen Brudia]], Valna fell deathly ill at a young age, driving her father into a sort of madness which eventually led to [[The Final Judgement]]. She is presumed dead as she was kept alive by a magical suspension maintained by her father.
